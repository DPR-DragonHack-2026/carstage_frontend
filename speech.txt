So we have created a Saas for car dealerships. Taking good images for car listings is an expense
both in time and labor. 
Our solution streamlines this process by leveraging a combination of
generative AI and a native mobile interface, which makes a process of creating professional
snapshots of cars simple, fast and cheap.

Generative AI can be an amazing tool, but is far from deterministic and may hallucinate parts 
of the image. Some of this riks can be mitigated by optimizing a system prompt, but that only takes
us so far.
Tradeoff is: creative flexibility for realiability.
A) We can pixel lock the car and use it as a mask, generate the background around it, but that skews 
the perspective on the image for some backgrounds.

B) We can let the model immerse the car into the background, but that sometimes results in hallucinated
features on the output.

We created a mobile interface that uses real-time on-device segmentation model, that lets a user
take an optimal picture of their car. This way we can tell the model to pixel-lock the car
and use the background in input as a sample to generate the output.

This way we get reliable generated images of the car in as fast as 12s per sample, with options
of choosing an arbitrary background and also rendering a dealership logo on a license plate.
